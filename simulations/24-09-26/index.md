# 🌊 CFD Simulation – NACA 6412 Aerofoil
 
<br>
 
**Date:** 24 September 2026
 
<br>
 
This section presents a **Computational Fluid Dynamics study of a NACA 6412 aerofoil**.
 
<br>
 
The simulation evaluates aerodynamic performance across a range of angles of attack, examining lift, drag, aerodynamic efficiency, and flow behaviour around the aerofoil.

## 📊 Key Results Summary
 
<br>
 
- **Simulation Domain:** External flow domain developed around the NACA 6412 aerofoil
- **Mesh Assessment:** Fine mesh result used to assess the resolved airflow around the aerofoil
- **Angle of Attack Study:** Aerodynamic performance evaluated across multiple angles of attack
- **Aerodynamic Efficiency:** Lift-to-drag performance compared throughout the simulated range
- **Drag Polar:** Relationship between lift and drag coefficients evaluated
- **High-Angle Behaviour:** Flow behaviour assessed at an angle of attack of 16°
